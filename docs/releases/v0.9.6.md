# FruitsAtelier v0.9.6

## Droplet editing and selection

- Configure TinyDroplet randomization strength and seed for each difficulty, enable or disable it for individual FSliders or in batches, and edit individual droplet positions with undo and project persistence.
- Use **Song Setup → Randomize droplets → Derandomize droplets** to keep TinyDroplets on authored paths. **Derandomize for HR Mode** compensates the Hard Rock random sequence instead.
- Set independent defaults in General settings for Legacy slider conversion and newly created projects.
- Selected objects now have bounding boxes for group dragging and horizontal scaling, including TinyDroplet bounds. Visible slider controls retain priority.
- Press **Shift+F** to open actions for the selected slider under the pointer. Stream and Stack patterns open on their matching dialog tab.

## Languages and settings

- The interface supports 13 languages: English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Russian, Spanish, French, Polish, Dutch, Filipino, Indonesian, and Thai.
- Language choices display native names. Selecting a non-English language shows a notice that its AI-assisted translation has not been proofread.
- Language preferences are in General settings. Settings pages use consistent typography, control spacing and compact Testplay background controls.

## Audio creation, export and synchronization

- Create projects from OGG and WAV files as well as MP3, preserving the audio format and optionally creating a linked osu! Songs difficulty.
- Imported maps require explicit export consent before the editor overwrites their original files.
- Corrected timing export after red-point edits and ignored General SampleSet differences during synchronization.

## Download and update

Download the [Windows x64 ZIP](https://github.com/frankhjwx/FruitsAtelier/releases/download/v0.9.6/FruitsAtelier-0.9.6-win-x64.zip), extract the entire archive, and launch **`FruitsAtelier.exe`**. Keep `Update.exe` and `current/` together. The package includes .NET and requires Windows 10/11 x64 with DirectX 11 support.

Existing updater-enabled installations can use **Settings → Application updates → Check for updates**, then download, save, and restart. Keep projects and custom skins outside the application's `current/` folder.

The `.nupkg` and `releases.win-x64.json` assets serve in-app updates. For manual installation, use the ZIP; its `.sha256` file verifies the download.
