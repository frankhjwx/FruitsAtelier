# FruitsAtelier v0.9.1

## What's new

- **Movement strain curve:** open the left sidebar to view whole-map Catch movement strain alongside the editor and Catch Preview. Click or drag the graph to seek; the curve shares the background calculation used for star ratings.
- **Display settings:** choose and retain the Windows display mode in General settings.
- **Editing controls:** Legacy Sliders convert automatically when edited. Droplet selection is locked by default, with a toggle and palette operation hints.
- **Settings layout:** skin selection now lives in Appearance, and Audio appears before Testplay keys.

## Fixes and improvements

- Fixed **Save and restart to update** doing nothing when clicked from Settings.
- Fruit placement replaces objects with matching start times and avoids unnecessary conversion work.
- FSlider endpoint dragging across time, slider selection, and tool state after undo and redo are corrected.
- Catch skins cycle colours per parent element and support nested font resources in lazer legacy skin exports.
- Default Catch circles are brighter, with corrected droplet and tiny-droplet proportions.
- Testplay volume bars accept left clicks. Editing and audio diagnostics provide additional information for investigating latency.

## Download and update

Download the [Windows x64 ZIP](https://github.com/frankhjwx/FruitsAtelier/releases/download/v0.9.1/FruitsAtelier-0.9.1-win-x64.zip), extract the entire archive, and launch **`FruitsAtelier.exe`**. Keep `Update.exe` and `current/` together. The package includes .NET and requires Windows 10/11 x64 with DirectX 11 support.

Existing updater-enabled installations can use **Settings → Application updates → Check for updates**, then download, save, and restart. Keep projects and custom skins outside the application's `current/` folder.

The `.nupkg` and `releases.win-x64.json` assets serve in-app updates. For manual installation, use the ZIP; its `.sha256` file verifies the download.
