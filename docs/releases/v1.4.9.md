# FruitsAtelier v1.4.9

This release improves note selection and editing in Hitsounding, remembers each difficulty's workspace settings, and makes shared difficulty imports more reliable.

## What's new

- **Hitsounding navigation and highlights.** With a note or sound selected, press Ctrl+Left or Ctrl+Right to select the previous or next sound-editable note, including slider repeats. Selecting notes highlights their sound tags. Press F to show or hide the notes associated with selected sound tags.
- **Whistle, Finish and Clap shortcuts.** W/E/R toggles these additions on selected notes and individual slider edges when the corresponding track is unlocked.
- **Per-difficulty Hitsounding settings.** The editor remembers track locks and mutes, the active tool, display mode, sound-tag labels, zoom, and play-line position.
- **Volume control across the editor.** Alt+wheel adjusts the selected volume channel over surfaces such as Catch Preview, dock controls, the bottom timeline, and Testplay. Dedicated zoom surfaces retain their zoom shortcut.

## Fixes & improvements

- **FAD collaboration imports.** Imports match renamed difficulties by their online BeatmapID and support data-only packages before a resource directory is available. Collaboration sections keep their time ranges. When a referenced difficulty is missing, the section becomes unassigned and the editor reports it.
- **FAD merge and resource recovery.** Merge review handles unavailable audio more reliably, checks for conflicts when applying resources, and can restore missing legacy resources even when the difficulty itself is unchanged.

## Download

Download the [Windows x64 ZIP](https://github.com/frankhjwx/FruitsAtelier/releases/download/v1.4.9/FruitsAtelier-win-x64.zip), extract the entire archive, and launch **`FruitsAtelier.exe`**. Keep `Update.exe` and `current/` together. The package includes .NET and requires Windows 10/11 x64 with DirectX 11 support. Embedded osu! Web requires the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime.

Use **Settings → Application updates → Check for updates** in an updater-enabled installation, then download, save, and restart. Keep projects and custom skins outside the application's `current/` folder.

The `.nupkg` and `releases.win-x64.json` assets serve in-app updates. Use the ZIP and its `.sha256` checksum for manual installation.

Mapset Verifier's corresponding source and build materials are provided separately as `FruitsAtelier-MapsetVerifier-v2.1.1-source.zip`.
