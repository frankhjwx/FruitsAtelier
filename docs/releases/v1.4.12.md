# FruitsAtelier v1.4.12

## Fix

- **Save all modified difficulties.** Ctrl+S now saves every modified difficulty in the project, including synchronized Song Setup fields and their linked `.osu` files. Pending export indicators remain visible until publication succeeds, and edits made during saving remain unsaved.

## Download

Download the [Windows x64 ZIP](https://github.com/frankhjwx/FruitsAtelier/releases/download/v1.4.12/FruitsAtelier-win-x64.zip), extract the entire archive, and launch **`FruitsAtelier.exe`**. Keep `Update.exe` and `current/` together. Requires Windows 10/11 x64 with DirectX 11 support.

Mapset Verifier corresponding source is supplied separately as `FruitsAtelier-MapsetVerifier-v2.2.0-source.zip`.
