# FruitsAtelier v1.3.1

## What's new

- **Adjustable editor layout:** drag the upper timeline and bottom overview dividers to change their heights, and drag side dock boundaries to adjust their widths. Double-click the bottom overview divider or either side dock divider to hide that region; double-click again to restore it with its previous size.
- **Floating Timing window:** drag the F6 window by its title bar and drag any corner to scale it from 50% to 100%. Continue editing, scrolling and playing outside the window while its timing draft remains open.
- **Scalable movement panel:** drag any corner to resize the movement readout proportionally; its position and scale are saved with the workspace layout. Reset position and scale independently from its context menu.
- **Legacy slider droplet modes:** choose Follow map, Derandomize or Randomize in Slider Inspector for Legacy sliders, including mixed Legacy/FSlider selections, while retaining their slider representation.
- **Testplay AR adjustment:** use Ctrl+Plus / Ctrl+Minus to change AR by 0.1. Each difficulty remembers its preview override until the editor closes, without changing beatmap settings.

## Fixes and improvements

- Resume Ctrl+P-paused testplay without repeating the countdown.
- Show playback progress beside the timestamp and keep their text sizes aligned as the overview is resized.
- Add a collapse control for the overlay overview and scale compact transport controls with the overview height.

## Download

Download the [Windows x64 ZIP](https://github.com/frankhjwx/FruitsAtelier/releases/download/v1.3.1/FruitsAtelier-win-x64.zip), extract the entire archive, and launch **`FruitsAtelier.exe`**. Keep `Update.exe` and `current/` together. The package includes .NET and requires Windows 10/11 x64 with DirectX 11 support. Embedded osu! Web requires the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime.

Existing updater-enabled installations can use **Settings → Application updates → Check for updates**, then download, save, and restart. Keep projects and custom skins outside the application's `current/` folder.

The `.nupkg` and `releases.win-x64.json` assets serve in-app updates. For manual installation, use the ZIP; its `.sha256` file verifies the download.

The bundled Mapset Verifier uses the editor's .NET runtime. Its complete corresponding source and build materials are provided separately as `FruitsAtelier-MapsetVerifier-v2.1.1-source.zip` in the Release assets; this attachment is not required to run or update the application.
