# FruitsAtelier v1.1.4

## What's new

- **Library organization:** group and sort mapsets by title, artist, creator, mode, date added or recent visit. Create FA Collections, manage membership from mapset cards, and return to recently opened maps. Searches, ordering and expanded groups are remembered per category.
- **osu! Collections:** browse read-only collections from your configured osu! installation, with each collection showing its matching difficulties. Open a map's osu! webpage directly from its Library card.
- **Difficulty creation and map templates:** name a new difficulty, make an independent copy of an existing difficulty, or apply a saved template's HP, CS, AR, OD, base SV and SliderTickRate. Manage reusable templates, choose their Catch icons and drag to reorder them. The source-enable choice is remembered across restarts.
- **Cross-difficulty copy and paste:** transfer object selections between difficulties in the current mapset. Pasting keeps the target difficulty's settings and timing, preserves legacy slider paths and durations where representable, and supports undo.

## Fixes and improvements

- Collaboration synchronization retains source red-point timestamps, BPM and meter without creating red points at section boundaries. Sample transitions follow each source section, legacy slider durations are preserved, and the final section includes objects and timing at its end. Open AiMod reports refresh after synchronization.
- Confirmed compensated imports can retain unrecoverable legacy slider geometry while recovering supported sliders in the same map.
- Reduce coordinate-conversion and text-layout overhead during dense playback, and reuse cached Library ordering data.
- Improve Library group/sort control alignment and direct menu switching, and clarify difficulty creation controls and disabled states.

## Download

Download the [Windows x64 ZIP](https://github.com/frankhjwx/FruitsAtelier/releases/download/v1.1.4/FruitsAtelier-win-x64.zip), extract the entire archive, and launch **`FruitsAtelier.exe`**. Keep `Update.exe` and `current/` together. The package includes .NET and requires Windows 10/11 x64 with DirectX 11 support.

Existing updater-enabled installations can use **Settings → Application updates → Check for updates**, then download, save, and restart. Keep projects and custom skins outside the application's `current/` folder.

The `.nupkg` and `releases.win-x64.json` assets serve in-app updates. For manual installation, use the ZIP; its `.sha256` file verifies the download.
