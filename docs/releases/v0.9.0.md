# FruitsAtelier v0.9.0

## What's new

- **Timing tools:** edit red and green control points, tap tempo, audition samples, and resnap objects with undo. The Timing page adds a scrolling audio waveform, beat-grid ruler, BPM markers, and a metronome with Ctrl subdivision playback.
- **Settings and volume:** open Settings directly beside Song Setup. Audio levels and skin selection are available in Settings; the editor's Master, Music, and Effect controls support dragging, hover-wheel adjustment, and Alt+arrow shortcuts, including during testplay.
- **Navigation and shortcuts:** Alt+wheel zooms the canvas, bookmarks and timing markers appear along its axis, and quick Snap cycles through thirds, quarters, sixths, and eighths. Paused arrow seeking follows Snap grid lines; seeking during playback uses full beats. Explicit pauses align to the nearest Snap grid line when beat snapping is enabled.
- **Testplay:** start immediately with a configurable 0–5 second lead-in in half-second steps. Esc returns to the selected start position.
- **Beatmap imports:** read stable-compatible lazer v128 Catch beatmaps, alongside versions 12–14, while continuing to export version 14.

## Fixes and improvements

- Fruit streams support group selection and vertical dragging, with individual fruit editing on a second selection. Linked droplet dragging across slider repeats and overlapping stream endpoint selection are corrected.
- Completing an FSlider replaces coincident fruits at its head. Combo numbering and preview grouping refresh after edits, and clicking empty canvas preserves playback time.
- Timeline objects use chronological drawing layers and standard skin resources, with corrected slider borders and proportions.
- Editor shortcuts work with Chinese IME active and respect the active page, focused field, and modal dialog. Rapid consecutive clicks on Settings controls are handled correctly.
- Hitsound timing is preserved when the playback output is rebuilt. Timing waveform scrolling, sample audition, and control-point scrolling are improved.

## Download and update

Download the [Windows x64 ZIP](https://github.com/frankhjwx/FruitsAtelier/releases/download/v0.9.0/FruitsAtelier-0.9.0-win-x64.zip), extract the entire archive, and launch **`FruitsAtelier.exe`**. Keep `Update.exe` and `current/` together. The package includes .NET and requires Windows 10/11 x64 with DirectX 11 support.

Existing updater-enabled installations can use **Settings → Application updates → Check for updates**, then download, save, and restart. Keep projects and custom skins outside the application's `current/` folder.

The `.nupkg` and `releases.win-x64.json` assets serve in-app updates. For manual installation, use the ZIP; its `.sha256` file verifies the download.
